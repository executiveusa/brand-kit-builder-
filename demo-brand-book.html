Page 02 — Philosophy

We are part of everything

Partimos de la idea natural que somos parte de un todo, una materia que exige estar en armonía con su entorno, tomar cada uno de los elementos y hacerlos parte de nosotros para sentirnos plenos física, emocional, espiritual y mentalmente.

Nuestro espacio será un lugar sagrado dónde todo mundo puede llegar a reencontrarse, dedicarse tiempo y obtener bienestar integral en sus organismos.

"Cuando sabes donde vas, el universo te abre el camino"

Page 07 — Applications

In Every Context

The brand system extends across every touchpoint, maintaining visual coherence while adapting to medium and scale.

Print

Business Card

84 × 55mm, 350gsm cotton stock, blind emboss logo, edge painted in Arcilla.

Print

Letterhead

A4, 120gsm uncoated, single color Natural on Arena. Generous margins: 25mm all sides.

Digital

Website

Responsive, minimum 16px body, dark mode supported. Load time target: <1.5s.

Environment

Signage

Brushed brass with etched logo, backlit with warm 2700K LED. Minimum 300mm height.

Packaging

Product Boxes

Recycled kraft board, soy-based inks, cotton ribbon closure in Natural.

Digital

Social Media

Instagram 1080×1350px, Stories 1080×1920px. Always maintain 40px safe margin.

Page 08 — Guardian Rules

Quality Gates

Every brand kit must pass these gates before shipping. No exceptions. No shortcuts.

Steve Krug Compliance

Every page must pass the 5-second test. Purpose obvious immediately. One primary action per view.

Auto-Pass

Contrast & Accessibility

All text meets WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, UI components 3:1.

Auto-Pass

No Decorative AI Slop

No generic gradients, glassmorphism, or meaningless bento grids. Every visual choice has a reason.

Blocker

Font Licensing Verified

All fonts have confirmed licenses. Free fonts documented. Commercial fonts have purchase records.

Auto-Pass

Asset Provenance

Every image, icon, and texture has a documented source. No unverified stock. No generated fakes.

Blocker

Mobile First

All layouts tested at 375px width. Touch targets minimum 44px. No hover-only interactions.
