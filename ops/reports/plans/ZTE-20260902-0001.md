## Status
PLANNED — branch-scoped implementation authorized. Production deployment is not authorized by this plan.

## Mode
BROWNFIELD.

## Outcome
Turn `executiveusa/brand-kit-builder-` into the canonical, self-hostable Pauli Brand Studio product named **PARÉ**, operated by the **One Hands** orchestration agent.

PARÉ is not subscription SaaS. It is sovereign in-house software installed and adapted to a company. The customer owns its brand intelligence, files, deployment configuration, data and portable exports. Pauli may sell installation, customization, migration and optional maintenance without making Pauli hosting a technical requirement.

## Product hierarchy
- Studio: Pauli Brand Studio
- Product: PARÉ
- Product line: Sovereign Brand OS / installable in-house brand software
- Operator: One Hands
- Canonical memory: ICM
- Execution adapter: Darya/OpenHands-compatible workers
- Quality path: Design Guardian → Gauntlet → Proof
- Social publishing adapter: Postiz, server-side and approval-gated

## Hero law
Primary philosophy: subtraction. PARÉ should communicate that the work is close when there is little left to remove without harming clarity, trust, usefulness, accessibility or control.

Hero reference quote:
> “Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.” — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Product line: **Brand systems, reduced to what matters.**

## Target
1. Pauli internal studio users.
2. Authorized agents calling the studio through MCP, CLI, REST, folder-drop or local runtime.
3. Client businesses receiving an installed sovereign copy/customization.
4. Creative teams that need high-quality anti-slop brand systems, SVG identity assets, voice, SEO, social and delivery packages.

## Commercial value
SELL + USE.

Commercial motion:
1. sell an installation/customization project;
2. deliver the software and portable ICM brand intelligence to the client;
3. offer optional maintenance, campaign work and upgrades;
4. expose bounded plugins/APIs for other agents and systems.

No customer must remain subscribed to Pauli to keep using the installed product.

## Constraints / protected law
- Preserve the canonical ICM stages: `00_intake → 10_strategy → 20_design → 30_validate → 40_deliver → 50_publish`.
- Do not introduce another source of brand truth.
- One Hands orchestrates; Darya/OpenHands executes bounded worker tasks.
- Builders may not approve themselves.
- `Official` means canonical + validated + present in an approved delivery package.
- No production deploy or external social publishing without recorded human approval.
- No secret values in Git, logs, screenshots, prompts or portable exports.
- No fabricated metrics, testimonials, partnerships, research, SEO data, runtime status or publish receipts.
- Third-party projects are references/adapters unless an explicit compatible license and a concrete need justify code reuse.
- Reuse current Brand Studio interface/runtime conventions before adding dependencies.
- Keep the primary user experience outcome-first and hide infrastructure behind progressive disclosure.
- Accessibility, reduced motion, responsive behavior, rights/provenance, rollback and owner control remain release requirements.
