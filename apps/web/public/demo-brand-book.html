01 — Philosophy
“Partimos de la idea natural que somos parte de un todo.”

The narrative starts with meaning before decoration. The brand system should explain what the identity is trying to hold together before showing how it looks.

Sequence reference: KAKU brand-book structure in the repository.

05 — Guardian rules

Beautiful is not
enough.

Rights

Fonts and images must have verified provenance before release.

Accessibility

Contrast, keyboard access, readable hierarchy and mobile context are release requirements.

Protected items

Locked copy and approved brand decisions cannot drift silently during rendering.

Independent review

The creator does not become its own final judge.

Truth label: this specimen demonstrates the intended brand-book system. It is not evidence that a production Guardian suite or automated release pipeline has run.
