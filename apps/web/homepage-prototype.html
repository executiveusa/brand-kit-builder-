03 — Sovereign system

The intelligence
travels with you.

Folders are the contract.

ICM gives every stage one job, explicit inputs, explicit outputs, and a human or guardian check. Another compatible agent can cold-start by reading the filesystem instead of replaying your history.

Cloud is a convenience.

Supabase can handle identity, tenancy, job/session indexes, approvals, and sync. Vercel can host the human surface. Neither replaces the portable brand manifest and project files.

Any capable model.

REST, MCP, CLI, local folder, cloud worker, and Popebot-style chat resolve to the same contracts. The adapter can change without changing what the company knows about itself.

USB-ready intelligence.

A sealed project pack can sit inside Hermes USB or another local agent workspace. Secrets stay outside the pack; the intelligence remains inspectable and transferable.
