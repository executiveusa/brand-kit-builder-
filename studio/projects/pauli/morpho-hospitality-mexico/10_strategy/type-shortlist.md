# TYPOGRAPHY SHORTLIST — MORPHO HOSPITALITY INTELLIGENCE · MÉXICO

Status: **strategy shortlist only — no font is approved until territory selection.**

## Requirements

- Spanish support is mandatory.
- Maximum two families.
- UI legibility at dense operational sizes.
- Strong numerals and labels.
- Distinctive enough to avoid generic SaaS.
- License/provenance must be documented before delivery.
- Must work in browser, PDF, presentations, reports, and small labels.

## Territory A — Atlas Vivo

### Candidate A1 — Resist Sans + Projekt Blackbird

**Resist Sans**
- Role: identity / display / strong headings.
- Why: authority, compact rhythm, excellent information hierarchy.
- Risk: commercial license required.

**Projekt Blackbird**
- Role: body, UI, evidence, navigation, data.
- Why: Latin Extended, free, architectural, clear.
- Risk: can become too Swiss/neutral if layout does not carry the cartographic character.

**Fit:** strongest current pairing for Atlas Vivo.

### Candidate A2 — Projekt Blackbird only

One-family system using weight, width, case, tracking and scale.

Why:
- lower licensing risk;
- operational;
- allows the spatial/cartographic system to carry identity.

Risk:
- needs exceptional custom wordmark construction to avoid looking generic.

## Territory B — Cuaderno de Campo

### Candidate B1 — R8:Parlif + Projekt Blackbird

**R8:Parlif**
- Role: selected narrative/display moments.
- Spanish / Latin Extended support in registry.
- Commercial license required.

**Projekt Blackbird**
- Role: body/UI/evidence.

Why:
Human editorial observation + operational clarity.

Risk:
Too much serif use would push Morpho toward boutique-hotel editorial rather than intelligence.

### Candidate B2 — Flaviette + Projekt Blackbird

Warmer hospitality expression.

Risk:
Higher chance of category-copy / restaurant / luxury-hotel cues.

## Territory C — Pulso del Destino

### Candidate C1 — Resist Sans only

A strong mono-family system with aggressive hierarchy.

Why:
- metrics, signals, time, status;
- avoids unnecessary editorial contrast.

Risk:
Could become generic monitoring SaaS.

## Rejected as primary direction

- Inter — current inherited default; insufficient identity.
- Geist — common product default; no project-specific reason.
- Space Grotesk — common AI/startup signal.
- Manrope — familiar SaaS neutral.
- Aalto — free and visually distinctive, but registry does not verify Spanish coverage strongly enough for canonical use.
- Goku — category association leans tech/startup.

## Current builder recommendation

If **Atlas Vivo** is selected:

**Resist Sans + Projekt Blackbird** is the preferred premium system.

If licensing is not approved:

**Projekt Blackbird only** becomes the sovereign fallback.

This is not final approval. Rights verification remains a release gate.
