# LOGO CRITERIA — MORPHO

Status: criteria only. No official logo is approved before territory selection.

## Job of the identity

The mark must make Morpho recognizable as a destination-intelligence system, not as:
- a butterfly NGO;
- an eco hotel;
- a travel agency;
- a generic AI startup.

## Primary naming

**MORPHO**

Descriptor:
**Hospitality Intelligence · México**

The descriptor is secondary and may change by vertical or market. The core brand remains MORPHO.

## Logo system must include

1. primary wordmark;
2. compact mark/avatar;
3. horizontal lockup;
4. monochrome version;
5. 16px favicon proof;
6. light/dark proof;
7. Spanish descriptor lockup;
8. English descriptor lockup.

## Hard bans

- literal butterfly silhouette as the whole identity;
- leaf inside letter O;
- map pin + leaf;
- generic radar;
- AI sparkle;
- neural-network nodes;
- globe;
- hotel roof;
- sun/wave travel logo;
- faux indigenous geometry;
- copied Mexican craft motifs.

## Desired memory

The strongest identity should encode the selected behavior system.

If Atlas Vivo:
- spatial relation;
- locating;
- route;
- field index;
- layered evidence.

If Cuaderno de Campo:
- observation;
- notation;
- registration;
- margin / mark.

If Pulso del Destino:
- temporal change;
- cadence;
- interval;
- signal.

## Wordmark test

The word MORPHO must:
- work without the descriptor;
- remain legible at 80px wide;
- not rely on gradient/color;
- remain distinct in black/white;
- retain character without decorative iconography.

## Small mark test

At 16px the compact mark must still read as intentional geometry.

No detail that disappears below 24px is allowed to carry the identity.
