# PARÉ — Three creative territories

These are materially different strategic directions. They are not three color variations of the same layout.

## A — Editorial Sovereign
**Governing idea:** software as a serious design office, not a dashboard product.

- Grid: editorial rules, large asymmetrical statements, documentary/proof sections.
- Type: forceful neutral sans for product clarity + restrained editorial serif for philosophical/cultural moments.
- Color: warm paper/ink base; electric blue for action; rare signal red; evidence specimens may carry their own approved palettes.
- Image role: real brand artifacts and source evidence, not stock “creative team” photography.
- Motion: small orientation/reveal transitions only after static hierarchy works.
- Navigation: three plain-language destinations plus one action.
- Signature moment: subtraction quote as the opening philosophy, followed immediately by a real brand-system specimen.
- Customer effect: “This is a design institution I can operate, not another AI dashboard.”
- Risk: overlarge typography can become performative if copy/proof is weak.

**Current recommendation and homepage implementation candidate.**

## B — Quiet Instrument
**Governing idea:** PARÉ disappears so the company’s own brand becomes the interface.

- Grid: compact, modular, highly functional; large whitespace but smaller headline scale.
- Type: system/humanist sans with almost no decorative voice.
- Color: near-monochrome; client brand colors appear only inside project evidence.
- Image role: workbench previews, before/after governance state, file/manifest proof.
- Motion: near-zero; direct response and state transitions only.
- Navigation: outcome composer first; all advanced workflow information progressively disclosed.
- Signature moment: a live “what PARÉ knows / what it needs / what it will deliver” context summary.
- Customer effect: “The system is invisible and under control.”
- Risk: may under-communicate cultural/creative ambition on a public sales surface.

## C — Cultural Workshop
**Governing idea:** a contemporary workshop where identity, type, print, image, sound and digital systems are visibly made.

- Grid: changing editorial compositions by medium rather than one repeated page system.
- Type: stronger DARYA-led display pairings and typographic specimens.
- Color: neutral master canvas with project-specific material palettes.
- Image role: production detail, vector construction, print texture, type, motion frames, social crops and environmental applications.
- Motion: one crafted process reveal showing concept → system → proof → delivery.
- Navigation: Work / Method / Install.
- Signature moment: an exploded brand kit that recomposes into the portable ICM manifest.
- Customer effect: “This can make culturally serious work, not just organize files.”
- Risk: higher asset production cost and greater temptation to decorate the process.

## Recommendation
Use **A — Editorial Sovereign** for the first public/home product surface because the current brief needs equal parts cultural authority, commercial clarity and ownership proof.

Borrow B’s progressive disclosure inside the operational product. Borrow C’s production/workshop language for case studies and brand-book demonstrations.

Do not silently average the three into one generic interface. The current homepage is a **Candidate** implementation of A until the visual audit and owner review pass.
