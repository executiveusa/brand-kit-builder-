- Mandatory subscription or hidden hosting dependency.

- Copying Gucci, Prada, COLLINS, festivals or other protected brand identities.
- Treating generated raster logo concepts as official vector masters.
- Publishing to social or production from a public/browser-only path without the authoritative approval boundary.
- Secrets in repo, ICM, receipts, screenshots or portable handoff.

- Mobile, accessibility and reduced-motion behavior must pass.
