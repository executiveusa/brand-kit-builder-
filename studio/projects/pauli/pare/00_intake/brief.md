# PARÉ — Intake brief

## Owner
Bambu / Pauli Brand Studio.

## Outcome
Turn the existing Brand Kit Builder into a private, installable in-house brand operating system that Pauli uses internally and can install/customize for client companies.

## Product hierarchy
- Studio: Pauli Brand Studio
- Product: PARÉ
- Operating agent: One Hands
- Canonical context/memory: ICM
- Execution workers: Darya/OpenHands-compatible workers + bounded specialists
- Quality path: Design Guardian → Gauntlet → Proof
- Optional social publisher: Postiz via approval-gated public API boundary

## Business model
PARÉ is not positioned as mandatory subscription SaaS.

Pauli sells:
- installation;
- customization to the business;
- migration/intake of existing brand knowledge/assets;
- optional integrations;
- optional maintenance, operations and creative services.

The customer keeps the installed software, files, approved ICM brand intelligence and assets if ongoing Pauli maintenance ends.

## Primary users
1. Pauli internal creative/product teams.
2. Client creative/marketing/leadership teams that need governed brand intelligence without SaaS lock-in.
3. Authorized agents that need a callable brand system via REST, MCP, CLI or file/folder contracts.

## Jobs
- turn messy company context into a coherent official brand system;
- create/maintain production SVG identity assets;
- keep brand voice specific and human;
- weave SEO into brand/content architecture without keyword slop;
- create social plans/adaptations from canonical brand truth;
- optionally schedule/publish through Postiz after approval;
- package brand books, assets, tokens and developer handoff;
- install on owner-controlled infrastructure.

## Governing philosophy
Subtraction over accumulation. Complexity may exist behind the interface; the user should see the minimum interface needed to understand, act, verify and retain control.

Hero quote requested by owner:
> “Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.” — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Primary product line:
**Brand systems, reduced to what matters.**

## Design ambition
The work should be credible to fashion, film-festival, cultural-institution and executive audiences without copying protected identities. The system has a house method, not a house look.

## Hard requirement
Anti-slop quality is a product requirement, not marketing language. No generated asset becomes Official merely because it looks polished.
