# PARÉ — Positioning

## Category
Sovereign in-house brand software / installable Brand OS.

## Positioning statement
For companies that need a serious internal brand capability without handing their identity to another subscription dashboard, PARÉ is an installable brand operating system that turns company context into governed strategy, identity, production assets, voice, SEO, social and delivery packages because its canonical intelligence lives in portable ICM files, its workers are replaceable adapters, and consequential outputs must pass independent proof before they become official.

## Brand promise
A company can leave with a brand system it can understand, operate, move and keep.

## Differentiation
### Everybody zigs
- “AI brand generators” optimize for instant logos/templates.
- traditional brand engagements often end in static PDFs and scattered asset folders;
- SaaS brand tools can centralize work but make the dashboard/provider part of the operating dependency;
- social/SEO tooling is usually added downstream, creating new drift.

### PARÉ zags
- starts with company truth and strategy;
- keeps one canonical portable brand manifest and ICM project;
- treats production SVG, voice, SEO and social as one governed system;
- separates creator and critic;
- lets Darya/OpenHands/Postiz remain replaceable execution/publishing services;
- is installed for the business and remains usable without mandatory Pauli hosting.

## Primary audience
Founders, executives, creative/marketing leads, social-purpose organizations and culturally sensitive brands that value high craft, ownership and operational simplicity more than a cheap volume of generated assets.

## What people should feel
- understood rather than processed;
- confident that the system knows what is canonical;
- relieved that the machinery is handled;
- in control of consequential decisions;
- proud to hand the resulting brand system to serious collaborators.

## What people should remember
**The brand system belongs to the company.**

## What we refuse
- visual sameness as “best practice”;
- generic luxury styling;
- AI vocabulary as a substitute for product value;
- fake proof;
- hidden lock-in;
- unnecessary features;
- autonomous approval of consequential work.

## Commercial model
PARÉ is sold as installation/customization software and professional implementation. Optional ongoing services may include maintenance, upgrades, creative operations, campaign execution and integrations.

This is intentionally different from positioning PARÉ as a per-seat or mandatory monthly SaaS subscription.
