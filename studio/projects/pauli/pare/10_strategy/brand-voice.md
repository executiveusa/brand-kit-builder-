# PARÉ — Brand voice

## Who we are
PARÉ is sovereign in-house brand software by Pauli Brand Studio. We install and adapt it to a company so its brand strategy, identity, voice, search/social rules, production assets and operating knowledge can live in one governed system the company controls.

## Audience
Owners, executives and creative/marketing teams that want high-quality internal brand capability without generic generated output or mandatory software lock-in.

## Core message hierarchy
1. **Brand systems, reduced to what matters.**
2. We install and adapt PARÉ to the business.
3. The company owns its software, files and approved brand intelligence.
4. One Hands handles the workflow; humans keep consequential authority.
5. SEO/social/publishing inherit the same brand truth rather than becoming separate silos.
6. Optional maintenance exists because help can be valuable, not because ownership is conditional.

## Voice axes
- Direct over ceremonial.
- Cultured over trendy.
- Grounded over futuristic.
- Specific over impressive-sounding.
- Calm confidence over hype.

## Real phrases / locked lines
- “Brand systems, reduced to what matters.”
- “We install it. You own it.”
- “A house method. Never a house look.”
- “Ask for the outcome.”
- “The machinery stays underneath.”
- “Preview · no publish.”

## Never say or do
- “revolutionary AI branding platform”
- “elevate your brand”
- “unlock your brand potential”
- “cutting-edge solutions”
- “luxury” as a generic self-description
- “fully autonomous” when an approval gate still exists
- “SEO optimized” without naming the actual checks/evidence
- “production-ready” without runtime proof
- “official” for unapproved generated work
- fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake metrics, fake clients or borrowed prestige

## Claims we can make from the current build
- PARÉ uses the Brand Studio's existing ICM stage architecture.
- One Hands has versioned workflow/request/receipt contracts in this repository.
- The repository contains CLI, MCP and REST interface implementations.
- PARÉ has a self-host Docker/Compose path in this build branch.
- Postiz scheduling/publishing is designed to fail closed without recorded approval.

Do not upgrade these claims to live production availability until the branch passes CI/preview/runtime verification and is deliberately released.

## Website writing rules
- Headlines should carry one thought.
- Body copy explains what the company gets and keeps.
- Prefer nouns and verbs over adjective stacks.
- Avoid feature-list sprawl; group capabilities by user outcome.
- Put proof near consequential claims.
- Explain technical infrastructure only after the product promise is clear.

## Sales rule
The offer is installation/customization, not rented access.

A good sales sentence names:
- what will be installed;
- what will be customized;
- what the client owns at handoff;
- what optional support may continue;
- what evidence defines acceptance.

## Social rule
Platform copy may adapt format and cadence, but core naming, claims, category, offer and approved proof remain consistent with the brand manifest.

SEO/social keywords never overrule natural brand voice.
