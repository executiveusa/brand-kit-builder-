# Montage — Project Lock

**Mode:** Brownfield product brand / launch system

**Outcome:** Launch Montage as an ownable AI-assisted video storytelling product that turns scattered clips into concise, emotionally coherent edits.

**Primary customer:** Creators, founders, small teams, storytellers, and operators who have useful footage but do not want to become professional editors to communicate an idea.

**Primary action:** Start a Montage.

**Customer job:** Bring in clips, let the system find the strongest moments, shape them into a compressed story, review the result, and export it in the format needed.

**Protected product truth:**
- Google Drive and OneDrive can act as source libraries.
- Originals remain protected; editing happens on working copies/proxies/derivatives.
- Montage can search, organize, transcribe, select, assemble, render, verify, and export derivatives.
- Sage/Bionic can assist locally with private editorial reasoning.

**Governing creative idea:** **Many moments. One story.**

**Primary brand behavior:** Separate fragments enter the system and resolve into a single composed sequence.

**Definition used in product:**

> **montage**  
> /mänˈtäZH/  
> A montage is an artistic technique or process of combining multiple separate images, video clips, or pieces of media into a single, unified composition.

**What we refuse:**
- generic AI-dashboard styling;
- fake film nostalgia;
- excessive film-strip clichés;
- feature walls;
- decorative gradients with no role;
- “magic AI” language without concrete outcomes;
- destructive editing of source masters;
- clutter that makes users feel they must learn editing software before creating.

**Success proof:**
1. A visitor understands the product in seconds.
2. The hero visually demonstrates montage rather than merely describing it.
3. The identity works as mark, wordmark, favicon, watermark, UI icon, social avatar, and video end-card.
4. The primary workflow is obvious: Bring clips → Find moments → Build montage → Export.
5. Product UI and brand kit share one token system.
6. SVG masters contain real vector geometry and no raster embeds.
