# Montage — Brand Voice

## Voice principle
**Editorial clarity with cinematic confidence.**

Montage should sound like an excellent editor: observant, concise, decisive, calm, and focused on story.

## Tone
- Clear before clever.
- Specific before abstract.
- Human before technical.
- Confident without hype.
- Cinematic without becoming theatrical.
- Minimal without becoming cold.

## Preferred language
Use verbs that describe what actually happens:
- bring in;
- find;
- shape;
- cut;
- compress;
- sequence;
- review;
- export;
- share.

Prefer:
- “Find the strongest moments.”
- “Build a 30-second story.”
- “Review before export.”
- “Your originals stay untouched.”

Avoid:
- “AI magic”;
- “revolutionize your content”;
- “next-generation creativity”;
- “effortless synergy”;
- technical architecture on primary marketing surfaces;
- claims of completion without proof.

## Core messaging
**Primary line:** Many moments. One story.

**Product line:** Turn raw clips into a finished montage without becoming an editor.

**Hero definition:**

**montage**  
/mänˈtäZH/

A montage is an artistic technique or process of combining multiple separate images, video clips, or pieces of media into a single, unified composition.

## CTA system
Primary: **Start a Montage**

Secondary: **Watch Demo**

Contextual:
- Bring in clips
- Find moments
- Review cut
- Export
- Open Media Library
- Continue locally

## Error voice
State what happened and what remains safe.

Good: “The proxy failed. Your original is unchanged. Try again.”

Bad: “Oops! Something went wrong.”
