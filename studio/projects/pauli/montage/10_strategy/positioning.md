# Montage — Positioning

## Category
AI-assisted video storytelling and montage creation.

## Positioning statement
For creators and teams with more footage than editing time, **Montage** is the video storytelling workspace that compresses many moments into one clear story, because it combines protected cloud ingest, media intelligence, editorial assistance, deterministic editing, and format-aware export in one workflow.

Unlike traditional editors that begin with a blank timeline, Montage begins with **intent**: what story are you trying to tell, what footage supports it, and what is the shortest complete version worth sharing?

## Core promise
**Many moments. One story.**

## Product sentence
Turn raw clips into a finished montage without becoming an editor.

## What the visitor should remember tomorrow
Montage takes scattered footage and finds the story inside it.

## Strategic difference
1. **Story before timeline.** The system starts from the intended outcome rather than an empty editing canvas.
2. **Protected masters.** Source footage remains untouched; Montage works on copies, proxies, and derivatives.
3. **Cloud-to-local continuity.** Google Drive, OneDrive, and local workspaces can feed the same media library.
4. **Compressed communication.** The product is intentionally strong at short, high-signal storytelling: 15s, 30s, 60s, 90s, walkthroughs, highlights, and social cuts.
5. **Human review at the consequential moment.** AI assembles; the human verifies before publish/export.

## Primary use cases
- social reels and short-form storytelling;
- event and travel montages;
- product or project walkthroughs;
- before/after and transformation stories;
- highlight reels;
- documentary selects and rough cuts;
- compressed explainers and proof-of-work recaps.

## Brand memory
Not “AI video editor.”

**A story-making system for turning fragments into a whole.**
