# Direction 01 — Proof Mark + Wordmark

## Idea
The brand should look like a **decision system with a pulse**, not a technology company trying to look futuristic.

## Mark
Use the existing geometric V as the seed. Its job is to mean **VIBE / verify / selected** rather than become a decorative tech icon.

Rules:
- Primary mark: sharp geometric V, no enclosing gradient orb.
- Proof Acid on Ink is the signature digital state.
- Ink on Warm Paper is the editorial/print state.
- Reject Red never replaces the primary mark; it is reserved for critique/status interactions.

## Wordmark
`VIBE ENGINEERING` should be direct, compact, and editorial. The identity is carried by spacing, scale, framing, and motion rather than distressed effects.

## Signature device
**THE PROOF FRAME** — a thin rectangular/system-line frame used to show what has been selected, verified, or moved forward.

- Proof Acid frame = selected / verified.
- Reject Red circle-slash or strike = rejected / needs work.
- Warm Paper line = neutral system structure.

## Why it fits
The system makes the brand's belief visible: AI can propose many things; humans decide what moves forward.
