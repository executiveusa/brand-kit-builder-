## Direction encoded in the system
- documentary urban creator environment without fake street styling
- near-black + warm-paper world
- acid green is the selected/proof signal
- reject red is functional, not decorative
- show people making decisions, not posing with AI
- negative space reserved for web copy
- no graffiti, torn paper, stickers, scratches, purple gradients, cyberpunk, holograms, glassmorphism, generic SaaS dashboards, or AI-brain imagery

## Production rule
The permanent VIBE brand board is `vibe-brand-board-v1.svg`. Generated photographic/video references are temporary until selected media is copied into owner-controlled storage with rights/provenance recorded.
