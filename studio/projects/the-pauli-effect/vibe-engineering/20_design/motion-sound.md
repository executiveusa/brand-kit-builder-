# Vibe Engineering — Motion + Sound

## Principle
Motion demonstrates a decision. It does not decorate a page.

## Signature sequence
**OPTIONS → REJECT → PAUSE → VERIFY → MOVE FORWARD**

This sequence should become recognizably VIBE across the website, intro film, social reels, presentations and product education.

## Song-led hero
The owner-supplied VIBE track is the timing source for the hero film. Visual edits follow meaningful hits and rests rather than arbitrary 500ms UI animation loops.

### Key behavior
- Build pressure with fast AI-generated/default options.
- Land on **LOOK FAMILIAR?**
- Hold visual motion during the musical pocket for effect.
- On the next strong hit: **VERIFY.**
- After VERIFY, the image world becomes calmer, more human and more intentional.

## Brand-kit scene
One early post-VERIFY scene must show a real person using the **actual VIBE brand system**:
1. Brand kit open.
2. Two approved type choices/weights shown, not a random font browser.
3. Person tests `VERIFY IT BEFORE EVERYTHING.`
4. Rejects one treatment.
5. Selects the intentional treatment.
6. Proof Acid frame confirms the decision.

The scene demonstrates taste without explaining the word “taste.”

## Motion rules
- Transform + opacity first.
- Hard cuts are allowed when the beat earns them.
- Freeze frames are preferred to decorative parallax when making a point.
- Red mark appears quickly and leaves; it is critique, not persistent decoration.
- Proof Acid may linger after verification.
- Reduced-motion users get static chapter states with the same narrative order.
- Never autoplay audible media.
