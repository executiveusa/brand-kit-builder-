# Vibe Engineering — Protected Items and Hard Bans

## Protected
1. **V.I.B.E. = Verify It Before Everything.**
2. Vibe Engineering remains owned by The Pauli Effect and part of the Pauli suite.
3. The audience is not required to identify as a developer or engineer.
4. Human judgment, taste, proof, systems thinking, and ownership stay central.
5. Public language stays shorter and simpler than internal methodology language.

## Hard bans
- No generic purple/blue AI gradients.
- No AI brains, robots, matrix-code backgrounds, or fake futuristic-city shorthand.
- No floating-dashboard hero or meaningless bento grid as the default composition.
- No glassmorphism stack, nested card soup, fake metrics, or placeholder testimonials.
- No forced slang or imitation of a culture to sound current.
- No graffiti, distressed raster type, paint scratches, torn paper, sticker clutter, or red slash marks merely to signal “urban.” Those devices are allowed only when the concept specifically earns them.
- No more than two font families in the brand system.
- No font may be distributed without verified license/provenance.
- No acid-green text on warm-paper backgrounds; contrast is insufficient.
- No red as a decorative brand accent. Red means reject, error, critique, or needs-review.
- No copying another studio's visual identity. External work may set a quality bar, never the skin.
