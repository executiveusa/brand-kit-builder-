# Vibe Engineering — Intake Brief

## VERIFIED
- Brand name: Vibe Engineering.
- V.I.B.E. means **Verify It Before Everything**.
- Owner: The Pauli Effect.
- Relationship: part of the Pauli suite of products.
- Existing product repository: `executiveusa/vibe-engineering`.
- Existing visual seed: near-black, warm paper, acid-green proof signal, oversized statements, visible system lines, reduced-motion support.

## CLIENT-STATED
- Audience must understand the idea without technical credentials.
- The brand should teach systems thinking, critical thinking, discipline, first principles, and better decision-making without sounding complex.
- AI is part of the process, but the larger skill is how to think, choose, test, and improve.
- The brand should feel culturally current and urban without forced slang or fake street styling.
- VIBE should help younger people take ideas further, not simply teach AI tools.
- Public communication should be shorter, clearer, and less technical than the internal method.

## SINGLE BRAND PROMISE
You do not need to know code to build with AI. You need a process for deciding what good is, checking the work, and making it yours.

## JOB TO BE DONE
Turn AI from a one-shot generator into a tool people can direct with intent, taste, systems thinking, and proof.

## UNRESOLVED
- Final legal/trademark status of the VIBE mark and wordmark.
- Final font-file provenance package before redistribution.
- Final bilingual rollout timing.
