# Collins-Level Skill

Purpose: judge strategic confidence and creative direction at a world-class bar without copying Collins visual styling.

## Laws
- Strategy before styling.
- Self-evidence before experimentation.
- Content before containers.
- Proof before claims.
- One governing idea beats a collage of trends.
- Do not lead with model names, agent counts, or infrastructure.
- Reject generic SaaS defaults: floating dashboard hero, meaningless bento grids, gradient AI brains, endless rounded cards.

## Judge
Score 0–10:
- governing idea
- strategic clarity
- distinctiveness
- editorial craft
- cultural intelligence
- visual hierarchy
- conversion clarity
- responsive coherence
- accessibility
- system coherence

Minimum primary-flow score: 9.0.

## Output
Return: scorecard, strongest idea, single biggest gap, evidence for the judgment, and exact owning stage for repair. Never rewrite the work while acting as critic.
