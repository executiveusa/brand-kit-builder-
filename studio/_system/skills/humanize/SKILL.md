# Humanize / Anti-AI-Prose Skill

Purpose: remove generic AI-writing patterns while preserving the approved meaning, evidence, regional language and brand voice.

Use after strategy/voice facts exist and before public-facing copy becomes a release candidate.

## Source priority
1. approved brand voice and real writing samples;
2. verified facts/claims ledger;
3. customer/founder language;
4. channel/platform rules;
5. this cleanup skill.

A cleanup rule may never override a real writer's intentional voice habit.

## Process
1. Mark every factual claim and its source.
2. Identify generic AI patterns: inflated importance, vague sources, unsupported superlatives, sales filler, repetitive structures, abstraction, forced triads, canned contrasts, excessive headings, chatbot throat-clearing and synonym cycling.
3. Rewrite more simply while keeping each supported claim.
4. Preserve uncertainty where the source is uncertain.
5. Restore concrete nouns, actors and actions.
6. Read for rhythm; do not make every sentence identical.
7. Run the brand-specific `NEVER_SAY_OR_DO` and approved-phrases checks.

## Hard bans
- do not invent facts, names, numbers, dates, quotes or citations;
- do not create fake personality through made-up anecdotes;
- do not replace local/regional voice with generic corporate English;
- do not use luxury adjectives as a substitute for craft;
- do not add urgency unless it is real;
- do not trash competitors to create differentiation;
- do not change a legal/technical meaning for style.

## Output statuses
- rewritten copy remains Draft/Candidate until its source claims and voice are verified;
- a cleaner sentence is not automatically a truer sentence;
- no copy becomes Official until the parent brand package passes its normal gates.
