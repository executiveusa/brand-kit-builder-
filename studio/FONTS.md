# PARÉ font reference gate

Font selection is part of every brand-kit build. PARÉ must return:

1. A primary typeface, a secondary typeface, and a system-safe fallback stack.
2. The role and required weights for each pick.
3. Proof at display, body, caption, social-avatar, and 16 px favicon-adjacent sizes where lettering appears.
4. A current license read-back from the font owner or distributor before production use.
5. The owner's selected type direction recorded in the decision ledger.

Research starts with the two owner-specified Awwwards sources:

- https://www.awwwards.com/awwwards/collections/free-fonts/
- https://www.awwwards.com/best-free-fonts.html

The word “free” is not a license. The Awwwards article says its set mixes fully free fonts, personal-use-only fonts, trials, demos, and fonts with a free version. PARÉ may reference the collection and shortlist candidates, but must not bundle a font or claim commercial-use rights until the current original license is checked.
