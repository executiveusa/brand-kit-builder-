# Templates context

`studio/_templates/` contains blank, reusable starting structures. Templates are factory assets; they are copied into `studio/projects/<tenant>/<project>/` and then become tenant-specific product work.

## Routes
- `brand-project/CONTEXT.md` — required contract for a new brand project template.

## Law
- Templates contain no client-specific facts, credentials, or assets.
- Template changes require repository review because they affect future projects.
- Once copied into a tenant project, project-stage law governs the copy.
